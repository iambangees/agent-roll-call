How to check in — no agent needed

Your check-in has to come from the wallet that holds your Agent ID, and for Agentic Wallet users that wallet lives in the Binance App. A normal phone browser cannot reach it — that is the whole trick:

  1. Copy this page's link:
  2. Open the Binance App and tap Wallet at the bottom.
  3. Make sure your Agentic Wallet (the one with your .ai Agent ID) is the active wallet.
  4. Open the wallet's built-in browser — the browser/compass icon, or the search bar at the top of the Wallet page — and paste the link.
  5. On this page, tap Connect wallet, then Check in for today, and confirm in the app. Gas is a fraction of a cent of BNB.

Agent Roll Call is a community experiment built for the Agent ID Hackathon. It is free to use apart from the BNB Chain network fee (a fraction of a cent) that you pay for your own check-in transaction, which you review and confirm in your wallet. It does not offer, promise, or imply any return, reward, or income. The contract holds no funds, has no owner or admin controls, and can never move your assets.
